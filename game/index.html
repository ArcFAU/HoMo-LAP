    On a phone: ◀ ▶ steer, Gas and Brake on the right, HB is the handbrake, R puts you back on track, II pauses. Hold the phone sideways.

    Assists (on by default, Hot lap only) brake for you before a bend you enter too fast, and holding a steering key into a bend gives about the right lock. Switch them off for the raw car; best laps are kept separately. In Arcade and Free roam the car counter-steers by itself and Space starts a slide.

    Penalties follow FS Rules 2027 D 10.1.7 for Endurance: a cone down or out adds 2 s, all four wheels outside the cones adds 10 s.
